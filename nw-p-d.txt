Nullwave — Pitch Deck Draft

Tagline: Building the Future of Voice

Slide 1: Title & Vision

Title: NULLWAVE

Subtitle: Building the Future of Voice

Tagline: Private, seamless voice interactions anywhere, anytime.

Slide 2: Mission & Vision

Vision Statement: "Building the future of voice. The future of voice is not possible without privacy."

Core Mission: Empowering professionals, travelers, and gamers to communicate freely, take confidential calls, and use voice-first AI interfaces anywhere in public without sacrificing privacy or disturbing others.

Slide 3: The Problem

Lack of Privacy on Calls & Meetings: Conducting confidential calls in high-density or shared environments (open offices, co-working spaces, cafes, public transport) leaves sensitive speech exposed to eavesdropping.

Noise Disturbance to Surrounding People: Speaking aloud in quiet or public environments creates acoustic friction and annoys people nearby.

Barriers to Voice-First AI Interfaces: As voice becomes the primary interaction model for AI, public adoption is hindered by privacy concerns and ambient noise.

Slide 4: The Solution

Voice Containment Mask: A wearable voice containment device that traps and absorbs speech sound waves inside the mask.

Complete Acoustic Isolation: Withholds speech from escaping the mask, allowing natural conversation without sound leakage or outward noise disturbance.

Slide 5: Product & Industrial Design

A Fashion Statement: Designed to look like premium, high-tech wearable fashion rather than weird or intimidating tactical equipment.

Unmatched Comfort & Safety: Ergonomically designed so that it is genuinely more comfortable and safer to keep wearing than to take off.

Personalized Style: Customizable colors, trims, and modular exterior components to seamlessly complement any outfit.

Slide 6: Key Features & Capabilities

Acoustic Voice Containment: Advanced sound absorption keeping spoken audio completely private within the mask.

Soundless Bladeless Cooling System: Whisper-quiet micro-bladeless fan technology delivering smooth, continuous airflow and thermal comfort without blade flicker noise or mic turbulence.

Real-Time Live Translation: Seamless bidirectional translation (and vice versa) delivered directly to your ears.

AI Meeting Notes & Transcription: Continuous automated note-taking, summaries, and transcriptions for daily calls and conversations.

Aromatherapy Capsule System: Customizable scent capsules offering fresh, personalized internal mask scenting.

Slide 7: Market Opportunity (TAM / SAM / SOM)

TAM (Total Addressable Market) — ₹4.72 Lakh Cr ($57B): 350M global hybrid knowledge workers, international travelers, and voice-active gamers facing privacy/noise constraints.

SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market) — ₹24,975 Cr ($3.0B): 18.5M users across India's top metro hubs (business pros), frequent outbound/inbound tourists, and urban competitive gamers.

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market / Yr-3 Goal) — ₹270 Cr ($32.5M): 200,000 active users (~1.08% SAM capture) driven by enterprise co-working partnerships, airport duty-free retail, and gaming creator drops.

Slide 8: Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Target Persona — Business Professionals & Consultants: Remote workers, traveling executives, and consultants who frequently take confidential client calls on the move.

Geographic Wedge — Metro Tech Hubs: Concentrated initial launch across India's key business centers:

Bengaluru (Tech & Founders)

Mumbai (Finance & Corporate)

Delhi NCR (Consulting & Policy)

Secondary Market — Travelers & Gamers:

Travelers & Tourists: Outbound/inbound travelers needing real-time translation and quiet transit calling in airports and trains.

Competitive Gamers & Streamers: Late-night multiplayer gamers in shared apartments who need voice noise containment without ambient room bleed.

Slide 9: Business Model & Monetization

Hardware Revenue:

Device retail price: ₹15,000 (~$180 USD) per unit.

Recurring Software Subscription (SaaS):

Tiered subscription for premium AI features (Real-time translation, automated meeting transcription, summary insights).

Consumables Revenue:

Recurring sales of customizable scented capsules for internal mask scent management.
